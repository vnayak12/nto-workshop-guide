Data Cloud — Meet the Unified Customer
8 – 15 min

Maya's data lives in 3 places: an e-commerce platform (browsing + online orders), an in-store POS (her hiking boot purchase), and a loyalty app (her Adventurer account). Data Cloud has already ingested, resolved, and unified them. You're going to see what that looks like.

5

Explore Maya's Unified Profile

  1. Go to App Launcher → Data Cloud.
  2. Click Unified Profiles in the left nav.
  3. Search for "Maya Park".
  4. Open her profile. Scroll to the Source Records section.
✅ You should see 3 source records collapsed into 1 unified profile: ecom_customers (email match), pos_transactions (name + phone match), loyalty_members (loyalty ID match). This is identity resolution at work.
  1. Scroll to Engagement History. Notice the outerwear browsing events — these are what power the agent's personalization.
  2. Scroll to Loyalty. Confirm: Tier = Adventurer, Points = 2,450.
6

Find the Lapsed Loyalty Segment

  1. In the Data Cloud left nav, click Segments.
  2. Find the segment named "Lapsed Loyalty — Browsed Outerwear".
  3. Click into it. Note the segment criteria:
RuleValue
Loyalty statusActive member
Days since last purchase> 90
Product category browsedOuterwear (last 30 days)
  1. Confirm Maya Park appears in the Member Preview.
  2. This segment is what Trail Guide will use to personalize its recommendations. When the agent sees Maya, it knows she's in this segment — and can act accordingly.
ℹ️ You didn't build any of this — it was pre-configured in your org. In a real implementation, your Data Cloud team would define these data streams, identity rules, and segments. The point here is that once they exist, the agent can use them automatically.
2
Agentforce — Build the Agent by Talking to It ⭐ Wow #1
15 – 25 min

Trail Guide already exists in your org as a shell with 2 subagents pre-wired. Your job: add the Product Discovery subagent by describing what you want in natural language. No form-filling. No drag-and-drop. Just tell the builder what you want.

7

Open Trail Guide in Agentforce Builder

  1. Go to App Launcher → Agentforce.
  2. Click Agent Builder in the left nav.
  3. Find Trail Guide in the agent list and click Edit.
  4. You'll see the agent canvas. On the left: the agent's instructions. On the right: its subagents and actions.
  5. Confirm you see 2 pre-wired subagents: Order Lookup and Loyalty Manager.
💡 The Canvas view shows a visual map. You can switch to Script view (top right toggle) to see the underlying Agent Script code — but stay in Canvas for now.
8

Add the Product Discovery Subagent — By Talking

  1. In the builder, click + Add Subagent → choose "Describe with AI".
  2. A chat panel opens. Paste this description:
Paste into the builder chat

Create a Product Discovery subagent that helps store associates find relevant products for a customer. It should search the NTO product catalog by category and keyword, filter by price range and availability, and return the top 3 matches with name, price, and a 1-sentence description. It should also check if the customer has any loyalty discounts that apply to the results.

  1. The builder will scaffold the subagent — watch it wire the pre-deployed SearchProductCatalog and GetLoyaltyDiscounts Apex actions automatically.
  2. Review the generated instructions. You can tweak the tone — e.g., add: "Always address the associate by name and keep responses under 150 words."
  3. Click Save.
✅ Trail Guide now has 3 subagents. The Product Discovery subagent is live and wired to the product catalog and loyalty data.
9

Activate Trail Guide

  1. Click Activate in the top-right of Agent Builder.
  2. In the activation dialog, confirm the permission set is set to NTO Workshop Users.
  3. Click Activate. Wait for the green confirmation banner.
⚠️ Activation can take 30–60 seconds. If it times out, wait 30 more seconds and try again — it may already be activating in the background.

4
Vibe-Code a Store Associate Dashboard ⭐ Wow #3
40 – 50 min

Describe a clienteling card in plain English. Agentforce Vibes generates the Lightning Web Component. You approve and deploy it to Maya's Contact record page — live, in the org, in under 10 minutes. No Apex. No XML. Just describe.

12

Open Agentforce Vibes

  1. Go to App Launcher → Agentforce Vibes.
  2. Click New Vibe.
  3. Make sure Plan Mode is on (toggle in the top bar — this shows you what it plans to build before it builds it).
13

Describe Your Clienteling Card

  1. In the Vibes chat, paste this description:
Paste into Agentforce Vibes

Create a Lightning Web Component called "NTO Clienteling Card" for the Contact record page. It should show: the customer's loyalty tier and points balance (large, prominent), their last 3 orders (date, product, channel — online or in-store), their top 3 recently browsed product categories, and the latest Trail Guide agent recommendation for this customer. Use NTO brand colors (green #2e6b3e, tan #f5f0e8). Make it look like a card with rounded corners and a subtle shadow.

  1. Review the Plan it generates — confirm it's creating an LWC with the right fields.
  2. Click Act to generate the code.
  3. Vibes will show you the generated HTML, JS, and CSS. Browse it — you don't need to understand every line.
  4. Click Approve & Deploy.
✅ Vibes deploys the LWC to your org and adds it to the Contact record page layout automatically.
14

See It on Maya's Record

  1. Go to Contacts → Maya Park.
  2. Scroll down to find the NTO Clienteling Card component on her record page.
  3. Confirm it shows her loyalty tier (Adventurer), points (2,450), recent orders, browsing history, and the Trail Guide recommendation.
💡 Want to tweak it? Go back to Vibes and describe the change: "Make the loyalty tier badge larger" or "Add a button to open a new Trail Guide conversation." Vibes will update the component.
ℹ️ You just built and deployed a production-quality Lightning component without writing a line of code. This component queries Data Cloud for browsing history, Loyalty for tier/points, and Agentforce for the Trail Guide recommendation — all in one card.

What's Next — Cart Abandonment Rescue
50 – 60 min

Your facilitator will demo this — no hands-on required. Watch how Trail Guide proactively reaches out to Maya when she abandons a cart, using her loyalty tier and segment membership to personalize the incentive.

ℹ️
What the facilitator is showing:
  • A Flow triggers when Maya abandons a cart with outerwear items
  • Trail Guide fires autonomously — no human in the loop
  • It checks Maya's segment (lapsed loyalty + browsed outerwear) and tier (Adventurer)
  • It generates a personalized email: mentions the specific product she browsed, applies a tier-appropriate discount, references her points balance
  • The email lands in her inbox within 60 seconds of the cart abandon
