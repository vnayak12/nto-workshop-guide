Build a Product Discovery topic for NTO's customer agent, explore the other 10 topics already wired up, then see your work invoked from Agentforce Coworker, the employee-facing AI surface.

Introduction
1

What You'll Build

Northern Trail Outfitters (NTO) sells outdoor gear across 11 product categories, from outerwear and footwear to camping and electronics. In this workshop, you'll extend Trail Guide, NTO's AI shopping assistant, by adding a Product Discovery capability so customers can search the catalog, get personalized recommendations, and explore products through natural conversation.

Terminology Cheat Sheet

Meet Trail Guide Agent

Trail Guide is NTO's AI shopping assistant. It's pre-configured with 10 subagents covering customer service, orders, loyalty, wishlists, store locator, knowledge, reviews, marketing, and guardrails — but it can't search products yet. That's what you'll build.

7

Open Trail Guide in Agentforce Builder

  1. Click the gear icon (top right) to go to Setup.
  2. In the Quick Find box, type "Einstein Setup".
  3. Under Einstein Generative AI, click Einstein Setup.
  4. Toggle Turn on Einstein and refresh your browser.
  5. In the Quick Find box, type "Agents".
  6. Under Agentforce Studio, click Agentforce Agents.
  7. Toggle the Enable button for Agentforce at the top of the interface.
Agentforce Agents list in Setup — you should see Trail Guide here
  1. Click Trail Guide in the agent list. It will open in the Agentforce Builder in a new tab.
Agentforce Builder — Trail Guide with Explorer panel on the left
8

Explore What's Already Configured

Take a minute to look around. In the Explorer panel (left side), expand the Subagents folder. Trail Guide has these pre-configured subagents:

SubagentWhat It Does
Customer ServiceHandles profile lookup, order history, case management, product returns
Loyalty and PromotionsManages loyalty points, tier status, discounts, promo codes, proactive offers
Product FAQ and KnowledgeAnswers product questions from the NTO knowledge base
Order Status and TrackingLooks up orders and shows items and shipping status
Cart and WishlistManages saved items and wishlist
Store LocatorFinds nearby NTO retail stores
Feedback and ReviewsHandles product reviews and customer feedback
Marketing and OffersManages alert preferences and marketing subscriptions
ℹ️ You'll also see Entry Point (the router that directs conversations), Off Topic, and Ambiguous Question (guardrails). These are internal components, not customer-facing topics.
⚠️ What's missing? There's no Product Discovery subagent. If a customer asks "what jackets do you have?", the agent has no way to answer. You're about to fix that.
ℹ️ Key concept: Subagents define what the agent can do. Each subagent has Actions, backend functions it can call (Apex code, Flows, or APIs). You add a subagent, and the agent handles the rest.
3
Build: Add Product Discovery

This is the main build. You'll pull a ready-made Product Discovery subagent from the Asset Library so customers can find products in plain language.

9

Create a New Version (Draft)

Trail Guide is currently on Version 16 (Active). You can't edit an active version directly; you need to create a draft first.

  1. In the top-right corner of the builder, click New Version.
  2. The header will change to Version 17 (Draft). You're now on an editable draft.
ℹ️ Notice that the Add Resource button (next to the search box in the Explorer panel) is now enabled. On an active version, this button is disabled.
10

Add the Product Discovery Subagent

  1. In the Explorer panel (left side), hover over the Subagents category and click the + icon that appears next to it.
Hover over "Subagents" and click the "+" icon — a small popup opens next to the category
  1. From the menu, select "Add from Asset Library".
The Subagents dialog opens, showing all available subagents you can add to your agent
  1. In the search box at the top, type "Product Discovery".
  2. You'll see Product Discovery appear in the results. Click Select, then click Add to Agent.
Search for "Product Discovery" — select it and add it to Trail Guide
✅ Product Discovery should now appear in your Explorer panel under Subagents, alongside Customer Service and Loyalty and Promotions.
11

Review What's Included

  1. Click on Product Discovery in the Explorer tree to open it.
  2. You'll see it comes with 3 pre-wired actions:
ActionWhat It Does
Search Product CatalogSearches products by keyword, category, and price — the core search engine
Get Customer ProfileRetrieves loyalty tier, points, and order history for context
Get Personalized RecommendationsReturns tailored product suggestions based on purchase history
ℹ️ You don't have to wire anything up. Because Product Discovery is a pre-built subagent, its actions are already connected, and the Apex code behind each one is already deployed in your org.
12

Commit Your Changes

  1. Because you're on a draft version, the top-right corner shows a Commit Version button. Click it to publish your changes and make them live.
  2. Wait for the confirmation. Trail Guide now has Product Discovery capabilities and your draft becomes the new active version.
⚠️ Committing can take 30–60 seconds. If it seems stuck, wait a moment; it may be processing in the background.

Explore the Full Agent

Product Discovery is just one of Trail Guide's 11 topics. Try prompts across the rest too: FAQs, order tracking, wishlist, store locator, loyalty, customer service, product reviews, marketing offers, and the guardrails that keep it on topic.

18

Test All Agent Capabilities

In Conversation Preview, try prompts from each topic area. The agent figures out which topic each one belongs to on its own.

🛍️ Product Discovery (the one you built)

Price Filter

Show me rain jackets under $200

✅ What to expect: The agent searches the Product2 catalog and returns matching products with names, prices, and descriptions. Price-filter queries only return items under the given amount.

📚 Product FAQ & Knowledge

Product Care

How do I clean and maintain my hiking boots?

Warranty Info

What does the NTO warranty cover?

Gift Card Policy

What is your gift card policy?

✅ What to expect: The agent searches Knowledge articles and returns detailed answers from published articles like the Gear Repair guide, warranty terms, or gift card policy details (denominations, expiration, refund rules).

📦 Order Status & Tracking

Order Lookup

What's the status of order 00000106?

Order History

Can you show me my recent orders?

✅ What to expect: The agent looks up real Order records and returns the order number, status (Activated), total amount, date, and a list of items. Asks for your email or order number if not provided.

❤️ Cart & Wishlist

View Wishlist

What's on my wishlist?

Add to Wishlist

Add the Ridgeline Hiking Boot to my wishlist

✅ What to expect: The agent manages saved items: viewing shows products with prices, and adding confirms the product was found and saved. It asks for your email to identify your account.

📍 Store Locator

Find a Store

Where's the nearest NTO store in San Francisco?

Store Hours

What are the hours for the San Francisco store?

✅ What to expect: The agent returns NTO store details: name, address, phone, hours, and special features (like "Gear rental available"). San Francisco matches the NTO Flagship store on Market Street.

⭐ Loyalty & Customer Service (pre-built)

Loyalty Points

How many loyalty points do I have?

Support Case

Do I have any open support cases?

✅ What to expect: Loyalty queries return your points balance, tier (Member/Adventurer/Explorer/Summit), and available discounts. Support case queries show open case details including status and subject.

⭐ Feedback & Reviews

View Reviews

Show me my product reviews

Submit Review

I want to leave a 5-star review for the Cascade Hardshell Jacket — it kept me warm on a freezing ridge hike!

✅ What to expect: The agent creates a Guest_Review__c record with your rating and comments. Viewing shows your past reviews with star ratings and dates.

📣 Marketing & Offers

Active Campaigns

What promotions are running right now?

Subscribe to Alerts

Sign me up for sale alerts on Outerwear and Footwear

✅ What to expect: The agent shows active Campaign records with dates and descriptions. Subscribing updates the Alert_Preferences__c field on your Contact with your chosen categories.

🚫 Off Topic (guardrail)

Out of Scope

What's the weather going to be like tomorrow?

✅ What to expect: The agent declines and points you back to the NTO topics it can help with, rather than answering something outside its scope.

❓ Ambiguous Question (guardrail)

Vague Request

Help me with my thing

✅ What to expect: Instead of guessing, the agent asks a clarifying question and suggests specific options (product help, orders, stores, etc.) so you can pick the right topic.
ℹ️ Every response comes from live data: actual products, orders, Knowledge articles, and store locations, not canned text. The agent routes each question to the right topic and action, and the two guardrail topics keep it focused and ask for detail when a request is vague.

Multi-Topic Conversation Flow

Now try a full customer journey: one conversation that moves across several topics. Copy each prompt in order, and the agent handles the switch between topics without any prompting from you.

Step 1 — Product Search

I'm looking for a waterproof hiking jacket under $250

✅ Expect: Product Discovery searches the catalog and shows jackets under $250.
Step 2 — Product FAQ

How should I care for a waterproof jacket? What's the warranty?

✅ Expect: Routes to Product FAQ, searches Knowledge for care instructions and warranty details.
Step 3 — Wishlist

Save the Cascade Hardshell Jacket to my wishlist

✅ Expect: Routes to Cart & Wishlist and adds the product. May ask for your email to identify the account.
Step 4 — Store Locator

Can I try it on in a store near San Francisco?

✅ Expect: Routes to Store Locator and returns the NTO Flagship on Market Street with hours and features.
Step 5 — Loyalty

How many loyalty points do I have? Any discounts I can use?

✅ Expect: Routes to Loyalty & Promotions and shows your points balance, tier, and available discounts.
Step 6 — Review

I love this jacket — let me leave a 5-star review! "Best purchase I've made all year, kept me dry on a rainy trail run."

✅ Expect: Routes to Feedback & Reviews and creates a review record with the rating and comments.
Step 7 — Marketing

Are there any sales coming up? Sign me up for Outerwear sale alerts.

✅ Expect: Routes to Marketing & Offers, shows active campaigns, and subscribes you to Outerwear alerts.
🎯 That's seven prompts across seven topics: product search, FAQ, wishlist, store locator, loyalty, reviews, and marketing. The agent picked the right action for each without you telling it which topic to use, and every answer came from live data.

Agentforce Coworker: The Employee View

Trail Guide is what customers talk to. Agentforce Coworker is what NTO employees talk to, and it can hand off to Trail Guide to get real answers, using the exact Product Discovery subagent you just built.

21

Turn On Agentforce Coworker

ℹ️ Switch tabs: You've been working in the Agentforce Builder. Now switch to the browser tab where your Salesforce org is open (or open a new tab and navigate to your org). You need to go to Setup for this step.
  1. Click the gear icon (top right) to go to Setup.
  2. In the Quick Find box, type "Einstein Setup" and select it.
  3. Find Turn on Beta Generative AI Models and switch it On.
  4. In the Quick Find box, type "Agentforce Coworker" and select Get Started with Agentforce Coworker.
  5. Click Get Started, then click Turn On.
  6. A confirmation dialog will appear asking "Turn on Agentforce Coworker?" — review the details and click Turn On to confirm.
💡 Coworker lives in the global search bar. It is not a separate app; it is built into the search experience employees already use every day.
22

Ask Coworker a Direct Question

  1. Click the App Launcher (9-dot grid icon, top-left corner).
  2. Search for and select the Service app.

Click the global search bar at the top of your org. Ask a plain-language question that pulls from CRM data directly:

Direct CRM lookup

How many open cases does NTO have?

✅ Expect: Coworker answers straight from Case records. No agent hand-off needed, since this is a simple lookup Coworker can resolve itself.
Case History Lookup

What's the history on the oldest case, and what should I do next?

✅ Expect: Coworker pulls up the oldest open case, summarizes its history, and suggests a next action.
Create a Follow-Up Task

Create a task to follow up with the customer by September 18th to update them on their order status.

✅ Expect: Coworker proposes a Task with the follow-up due date, linked to the customer/case. Click Confirm to create it.

✅ Expect: Coworker recognizes this needs Trail Guide, hands the request off, and Trail Guide's Product Discovery subagent (the one you built in Unit 3) runs the actual search and returns the answer, all inside the same Coworker conversation.

Compare the Two Entry Points

Trail GuideAgentforce Coworker
Who talks to itCustomersNTO employees
Where it livesEmbedded chat / Conversation PreviewGlobal Salesforce search bar (also Slack, Teams)
What it's built fromSubagents (Product Discovery, Loyalty, etc.)Orchestrates whole agents, Flows, CRM actions, APIs
What you builtThe Product Discovery subagent itself—

ℹ️ Key concept: Coworker does not call subagents directly. It calls agents (like Trail Guide). Trail Guide's own reasoning engine then routes internally to the right subagent. Coworker sees "Trail Guide" as one capability; it never picks the subagent itself.
🎯 The point: the Product Discovery subagent you built in Unit 3 is not just a demo inside one preview panel. It is a real capability other parts of the org, including a completely different AI surface, can call on.

Build a Product Insights Dashboard with Agentforce Vibes

In this unit, you'll use Agentforce Vibes, Salesforce's AI-powered development tool, to build something different: a visual Product Insights Dashboard that surfaces patterns across the NTO catalog you've been searching all workshop — like which categories have the most products and where prices cluster.

26

Launch Agentforce Vibes

  1. Select the Setup gear icon, then select Agentforce Vibes.
  2. Accept the Terms and Conditions.
  3. You'll see an "Agentforce Vibes is loading…" screen with the message "This may take a few minutes — leave this page open and we'll refresh when it's ready!" Wait for it to finish loading — do not close or navigate away.
⚠️ If you see an error: wait 2–3 minutes and refresh the page. If it's still stuck, try opening Agentforce Vibes in an incognito/private window.
  1. Once loaded, select I agree to the terms on the left sidebar, then click Enable and Start Building.
💡 Agentforce Vibes turns natural language into working Apex, LWC, and other Salesforce metadata. It understands the org you're working in, so it can see the same Product2 data you've been searching all workshop.
27

Prompt Vibes to Build the Dashboard

You're going to build a Product Insights Dashboard — a Lightning Web Component that visualizes the NTO catalog at a glance: product counts by category, price ranges, and a few standout items.

  1. In the left sidebar, click the Codey icon to open the Agentforce Vibes chat panel.
  1. In the Vibes sidebar, enter a prompt like:
Prompt for Vibes

Create a Lightning Web Component called Product Insights Dashboard that queries active Product2 and PricebookEntry records and displays: a bar chart of product count by category (Family), the average price per category, and a list of the 5 highest-priced active products. Use the Standard Price Book for pricing.

  1. Watch as Vibes plans the component before writing anything. It will typically explain what it's about to generate, including the Apex controller and the LWC itself, before touching your org.
⚠️ Stay at your screen: Vibes will pause several times and ask for permission to Run before it takes actions (e.g. creating files, deploying metadata). Watch for these prompts and click Run when they appear to continue with the build.
  1. Review what it proposes: an Apex class exposing @AuraEnabled methods to fetch the aggregated data, plus the LWC markup and JavaScript to render it.
ℹ️ Key concept: Vibes-generated code is a starting point, not a guarantee. Review the Apex controller the same way you'd review a teammate's pull request — especially anything touching SOQL aggregate queries.
28

Deploy and Add the Dashboard

  1. Once you're happy with what Vibes generated, tell it to deploy the component to your org:
Prompt for Vibes

Deploy this component to my org.

  1. Vibes will deploy the Apex controller and LWC directly to your org. Confirm the deployment succeeds with no errors before moving on.
✅ Expect: A "Deployment succeeded" confirmation showing both components created — an Apex Class (the controller) and an LWC Bundle (the dashboard component itself).
  1. Go to the Sales App, open Accounts, and select Northern Trail Outfitters.
  2. On the Account record page, click the gear icon (top right) and select Edit Page to open Lightning App Builder.
  3. In the left-hand component panel, scroll to the Custom section and find ProductInsightsDashboard.
  4. Drag it onto the Account record page layout.
  5. Click Save, then click Activate.
  6. Under App Default, select Assign as App Default.
  7. Under Lightning Apps, select Sales, click Next.
  8. Select Desktop, click Next.
  9. Click Save.
29

View Your Dashboard

Go back to the Northern Trail Outfitters Account record.

✅ Expect: A visual breakdown of the NTO catalog — products grouped by category, average price per category, and a short list of the highest-priced items — all pulled live from the same Product2 and PricebookEntry data Trail Guide has been searching all workshop.
💡 Try it your way: ask Vibes to add a filter (e.g. only show categories with 3 or more products), or a second chart, such as price distribution within a single category.
